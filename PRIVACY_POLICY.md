# StudyFlow — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

StudyFlow is a student study planner. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## What we collect
- **Account details:** your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name
  and email address from Google. We never receive your Google password.
- **Password:** if you register with an email address, your password is sent over HTTPS and stored
  only as a one-way **bcrypt hash**. We cannot read it.
- **Your study data:** the tasks you create (title, subject, notes, due date, priority, status), your
  study/focus sessions (start time, length, whether it was completed), and your XP and badges.
- **Settings:** your chosen language, theme and avatar.
- **Device notification token:** a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can send you the reminders you enable.

## How it is used
Only to run the app: to sign you in, keep your tasks in sync between your phone and our server, send
the reminders you switch on, and show your progress. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for
analytics or profiling.

## Where it is stored and who processes it
- Your data is stored on our server (Microsoft Azure App Service and Azure SQL Database).
- Google (Google Sign-In) and Firebase Cloud Messaging process sign-in and notification delivery.
- On your phone, data is kept in the app's private storage so the app works offline.

## Your choices and rights
- **Delete your data:** in the app go to **Settings → Delete my account**. This permanently deletes your
  account, tasks, study sessions, badges and device tokens from our server.
- You can switch off notifications in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.
- To ask a question, or to request deletion without using the app, email **[YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS]**.

## Security
Data is sent over HTTPS, passwords are hashed with bcrypt, and sign-in tokens expire after 7 days.

## Children
StudyFlow is intended for students aged 13 and older.
